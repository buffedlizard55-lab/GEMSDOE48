FPw=S−TPw INVERSION WITHDRAWN

Why the old 0.2778 inversion is invalid

The former DS48/H49/H55 narratives inferred hidden truth, thresholds, ceilings, and live-equivalent scenarios from owner-reported scores. Those inferences are not supported by the official metric.

No hidden-truth count, live-score gain/loss bracket, universal break-even threshold, or private-label projection is established by this archive. The 0.2778 attribution is not verified against the local TIFF bytes.

Official metric components

k(d) = max(1 − d/R, 0)
TPw  = Σ_g max_x p(x) k(d(x,g))
FPw  = Σ_x p(x) [1 − max_g k(d(x,g))]
FNw  = Σ_g [1 − max_x p(x) k(d(x,g))]
DTI  = TPw / (TPw + 0.2 FPw + 0.8 FNw + ε)

TPw uses truth-centred maxima; FPw uses prediction-centred residual mass. They are not generally complements. The historic substitution FPw=S−TPw (equivalently assuming prediction-centred credit equals truth-centred TPw) is not a general identity. Therefore live scores and emitted mass do not determine hidden truth density or an addition/removal threshold by that route.

The pointwise quantity 0.2 × DTI is at most a conditional local break-even calculation when marginal TPw gain equals prediction-centred self-credit in a restricted one-variable geometry. It is not a universal gate for arbitrary cells, groups of cells, or removals.

Reproducible scope vs withdrawn scope

  • Reproducible: official-grid bytes, file hashes, DS combination arithmetic, public-proxy measurements and historical build commands (when explicitly marked forensic).
  • Withdrawn: inferred private truth counts, score ceilings, fitted live calibration, universal thresholds, live-equivalent scenario bands, promotion gates, and local-file-to-leaderboard attribution.
  • Not claimed: score gain, private-label performance, organizer acceptance, or a verified 0.2778 local-file link.

The preserved registry receipt and old Gate-2 JSON files carry explicit invalidation labels. The obsolete DS48 site builder now refuses to regenerate these pages.

Numerical reproducibility does not validate an invalid assumption.
