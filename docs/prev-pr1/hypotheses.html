1H48-A: cross-scale potential-field maxspots
RTP magnetics, TMI horizontal/vertical gradients, isostatic gravity and slope		Connect persistent local maxima of horizontal-gradient magnitude across upward-continuation scales, then require gravity–magnetic edge agreement. Abrupt density/magnetization boundaries can reveal concealed basin faults beneath alluvium, where a surface-trace catalogue is least complete. Unlike prior thresholded Euler/ridge outputs, this targets scale persistence and connected maxspot topology using the 2025 USGS pymaxspots method.High / medium. Data are obtainable: the competition feature stack and official GeoDAWN release. Not implemented; therefore no slot.
2H48-B: radiometric alteration-edge asymmetry
K, eU, eTh, K/eTh plus detrended slope		Oriented phase congruency on K/eTh boundaries, retained only where opposite fault flanks show asymmetric alteration and no topographic ridge artifact. Fluid-rock alteration may make previously unmapped permeable structures visible even without scarps. Prior work used radiometric corroboration/population, not directional cross-fault asymmetry.Medium-high / medium. GeoDAWN radiometrics are officially obtainable.
3H48-C: strain–seismicity corridor breaks
dilatation, shear strain, second invariant, earthquake density		Tensor-invariant ridge intersections and change points along aseismic gaps. Distributed strain aligned across a low-earthquake segment can indicate a locked or blind continuation omitted from surface inventories. Differs from scalar strain weighting by using orientation coherence and along-strike changepoints.Medium / low-medium. Provided layers suffice.
4H48-D: alluvial scarp curvature polarity pairs
official 1 m 3DEP DEM, multi-azimuth openness, profile curvature		Paired positive/negative curvature at consistent offset across fan surfaces, robust across illumination azimuths. Subtle young scarps in alluvium are precisely where small traces may be absent from regional catalogues. Differs from ridge/openness detectors by requiring signed curvature-pair geometry and fan-surface context.Medium / high. USGS says 3DEP is free and without use restrictions; competition provides tile URLs.
5H48-E: conductivity-base rupture concordance
surface conductivity, depth to conductive base, gravity edge		Co-located lateral conductivity gradient and conductive-base curvature break along a gravity edge. A buried permeable fault may juxtapose basin fill and alter fluids without a mapped surface trace. Differs from prior clay-cap breach ideas by requiring depth-surface rupture geometry rather than low conductivity alone.Medium-low / low. Provided feature stack suffices.

Validation decision

H48-A is the best new geological detector but has not been trained or spatially held out, so it is not slot-cleared. The urgent H48 fusion requested in the brief is a separate, frozen meta-hypothesis over two existing families. It passed the four-block SGMC proxy (+0.00893 mean DTI, 4/4 positive) but remains proxy-only. No automated competition submission was made.

The free-source availability check is documented on the source register. External-data use must still satisfy the competition rules and sponsor-sharing requirement.
