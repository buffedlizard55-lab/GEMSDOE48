Evidence model

For source confidence x and reliability discount r:

m(F) = r x
m(not F) = r (1-x)
m(Θ) = 1-r

The two basic assignments are multiplied conjunctively. Conflict is K = m1(F)m2(not F) + m1(not F)m2(F). Yager's rule assigns K to Θ. The submission is min–max-normalized Bel(F)=m(F); the diagnostic stores m(Θ). Discounts 0.90 (dotted) and 0.85 (tip) were fixed before proxy scoring and encode only family ordering—not calibrated probabilities.

The binary source cases produce submission values 0 (neither), about 0.0863 (tip only), 0.1371 (dotted only), and 1 (agreement). This strongly protects agreement while retaining weaker exclusive evidence. A naïve mean would emit 0, 0.5, and 1.

Three verification passes

  1. Implementation: SHA-256-pin parents/template; assert equal CRS, transform, and shape; combine; write all-finite float32; independently re-open.
  2. Bug/edge review: test mass conservation, no conflict under agreement, invalid-range rejection, footprint handling, and no nodata sentinel.
  3. Request re-check: verify unique hash, not-a-mean check, four spatial proxy blocks, diagnostic layer, one-click primary download, name/note, and source register.

Irregularities and hard limits

  • Rule mismatch: official text requests null/NaN outside bounds, but the owner reports the portal rejected NaNs as outside [0,1]. The primary is all-finite zero outside, following the observed validator behavior. Portal acceptance is not tested here.
  • Score attribution: 0.2778 and 0.2632 are owner-supplied score-to-family associations. Public leaderboard rows do not reveal file hashes. Treat exact artifact attribution as unverified.
  • Validation: SGMC off-catalogue faults are not hidden expert labels. Spatial blocking reduces geographic leakage but does not remove shared compilation/source bias.
  • Evidence independence: the two families are independently built but overlap 31,614 cells and may share upstream features. Dempster-style combination assumes evidence independence more strongly than can be demonstrated.
  • Current target: official leaderboard was 0.3774 at review, not 0.3195. It can change.
  • Access: this environment can research public sources and build/test files. It has no user's DrivenData session and cannot spend a weekly submission slot or observe a private score. GPU training was not needed for this fusion.
