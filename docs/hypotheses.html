H57-A frozen test and result

Fit five-iteration Huber IRLS K = intercept + slope × Th, calculate robust residual z, require z≥2 and nonzero TMI-up150 gradient at or above valid-footprint p75, rank by z×gradient, and add up to 2,000 selected cells to the frozen H49 reference. H49-positive cells are ineligible; there is no catalogue-distance filter. The 2,000-cell addition screen used no catalogue geometry or proxy truth for feature construction or ranking.
