Faults can truncate or juxtapose units without appearing in a Quaternary-fault inventory. Proposed analysis is of mapped unit-contact topology, not rasterizing SGMC fault lines as predictions; scale and source-map lineage matter. Potentially moderate, high uncertainty; medium–high cost. 3 · H48-3: OPERA InSAR gradients NASA/JPL OPERA DISP-S1 LOS displacement trends, robust temporal change points, coherence masks, across-line gradients. A creeping or active fault may generate a persistent narrow deformation gradient without a mapped scarp. Persistence controls noise; locked or inactive faults can be missed. No InSAR time-series workflow is documented in the reviewed local campaign set; this is not a global-novelty claim. Low–moderate public-label DTI potential; high cost. Official product metadata checked; Earthdata Login required; no granules or exact overlap counts downloaded.
4 · H48-4: channel-profile breaks USGS NHDPlus HR / 3DHP channels and 3DEP elevation; longitudinal knickpoints, paired profile breaks, and lateral channel deflection conditioned on slope/lithology. Fault displacement may offset or steepen streams where scarps are subtle or eroded. Multiple aligned channels can reduce single-channel noise. Distinct from valley-chain/ridge occupancy: targets profile discontinuities and paired channel response. Low–moderate, terrain-dependent; medium cost. Official hydrography and 3DEP pages checked; exact in-footprint coverage unaudited.
5 · H48-5: geothermal favorability + spread USGS Great Basin favorability percentiles from 1,200 realizations, GeoDAWN footprint, and distance to known catalogue; high median plus high ensemble spread used only as a structural search prior. Favorability may prioritize hydrothermal pathways missing from the inventory; uncertainty may identify underconstrained regions for review. It is not fault truth. Differs from reweighting the same input channels by using an independent regional model, subject to target-lineage audit. Expected public DTI unknown, likely low–moderate; high cost. Official USGS release metadata checked; ~1.22 GB package not downloaded; overlap not audited.

Promotion requirements

  • Audit official-data lineage, licensing, spatial coverage, map scale, missingness, and possible target/feature leakage before modeling.
  • Freeze the spatial folds and scoring domain before tuning. Rebuild any training-derived source within each fold; avoid using held-out labels in feature construction or proximity pruning.
  • Report paired fold results against the current blocked best, both input baselines and a simple mean; no pooled tier selection as a substitute.
  • A public-proxy improvement is necessary evidence at most. Do not spend a slot without beating the best under comparable blocked semantics and a defensible relationship to private expert truth.
