Dempster–Shafer terminology: ignorance is not conflict

m(Θ) is ignorance/unassigned mass on the frame Θ. K is conflict, the mass assigned to the empty set by the unnormalised conjunctive combination. They are different quantities. Under Yager's rule, K is transferred into Θ; therefore a Yager output may report a combined unassigned layer m(Θ) + K. That combined raster is not the separate raw-conflict raster.

The historical H49 file labelled unassigned diagnostic is described by its receipt as the Yager-combined unassigned layer; do not interpret it as a standalone K map. The separate belief field is likewise a historical diagnostic, not a score estimate.
