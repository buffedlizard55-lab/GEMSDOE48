Inputs and frame

Inputs are the public-owner-mirror H33-2-B2 dotted surface and H33-D tip/stepover surface. Their full-file SHA-256 values, byte counts, attribution limitations, and restore locations are in sources and provenance. Both are sparse binary emissions. They are not organizer-authenticated, and zero may mean “no candidate emitted” rather than an observation against a fault.

For each pixel, the model operationally treats an input value p as a binary opinion over the frame {F, not-F}, discounted by a fixed symmetric reliability ρ=0.5:

m(F)       = ρ p
m(not-F)   = ρ (1 − p)
m(Θ)       = 1 − ρ

This is an explicit modeling convention—not a learned probability, calibrated accuracy, or official competition model.

Combination and disagreement

For source masses m₁ and m₂, raw conjunctive conflict is

K = m₁(F)m₂(not-F) + m₁(not-F)m₂(F).

The normalized Dempster combination divides the non-conflicting conjunctive masses by 1−K. The submitted research candidate is the resulting m(F). We separately export:

  • Unassigned mass m(Θ) after normalization. This records residual ignorance; it is not the raw conflict.
  • Raw conflict K before normalization. Canonical Dempster normalization redistributes conflict, so K is a diagnostic layer—not mass retained by the normalized rule.

Not an arithmetic mean

The independent build receipt verifies that the primary output and simple 0.5 arithmetic mean are not equal. There are 47,905 differing in-footprint cells; full-footprint MAE is 0.00205494 and MAE on the positive union is 0.22166091. The maximum absolute difference is 0.25. Full-footprint Pearson correlation is 0.999355 because both surfaces are sparse with many shared zeros; union-only correlation is also structurally uninformative. Pixelwise inequality, mass values, and errors—not correlation alone—establish distinction.

Do not conflate the historical main-branch model

The upstream main branch included an alpha=.99 normalized-belief experiment and a full-confidence binary union decision. They are retained as distinct comparators. On the newer pinned SGMC derivative, the prior union mean DTI is 0.096992 and the current ρ=.5 belief mean is 0.069261; on catalogue labels they are 0.085538 and 0.032588. The older raw SGMC raster produces a separate sensitivity pair of 0.095957 and 0.068574. The union is rescored from the two pinned candidate inputs in the same fold domains. Earlier upstream quadrant numbers used full-grid predictions and are not directly comparable.

A separate Yager-rule evidence module and candidate TIFF are preserved as historical/alternative work; they are not used to build this Dempster candidate and are not slot-cleared. H53 three-source adaptive fusion (2026-10-07; separate candidate)

H53-1's frozen lidar build combines dotted H33-2-B2 and H36-1 rung30 kernel-credit beliefs (H36 is an H19-5/rung-30 repacking, not the actual tip/step-over family) plus native-lidar scarp belief, using canonical Dempster's rule, (A ⊕ B) ⊕ L, under a terrain-adaptive per-pixel lidar reliability map (0.75 smooth / 0.25 rough / 0.05 uncovered; contextual discounting after Mercier, Quost & Denœux, ECSQARU 2005). Its frozen graded/twin results fail the blocked gate (0.071408 / 0.089559 vs H49 0.100751), so no slot is cleared. The separate H53-RadEdge-1 candidate combines B2 with H33-D, the actual tip/step-over parent, plus weak radiometric edges; it also fails its gate. See the H36 classification erratum, H53-RadEdge one-click download, frozen lidar H53 slate, and lidar H53 results.

Grid and outside-footprint encoding

The candidate is one-band float32 on the 3,730 × 3,292, 100 m EPSG:32611 competition grid. The primary file is finite and bounded by [0,1] inside the derived survey footprint. Every outside cell is NaN and the GeoTIFF nodata tag is NaN, matching the public sample-template convention. The sample pixel values are never used as labels. The local validator confirms the finite mask exactly equals the derived footprint; this is not organizer acceptance.
