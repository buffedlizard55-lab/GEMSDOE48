Inputs are distinct evidence families

The two parent surfaces are a dotted-family B2 mask and an H33-D tip/step-over mask. Each was converted to a 300 m triangular maximum-credit support field using the official DTI distance kernel. Their masks share 31,614 cells (out of 37,654 dotted and 41,865 tip cells), so statistical independence is not established. This is a combination of two related/overlapping sources, not two independent confirmations.

Normalized Dempster rule and mass terms

For two sources over frame {F, ¬F}, with Θ as uncommitted mass, the unnormalized conflict is:

K = m₁(F)m₂(¬F) + m₁(¬F)m₂(F)

Normalized Dempster combination divides non-conflicting intersections by 1−K. For example, m₁₂(F)=[m₁(F)m₂(F)+m₁(F)m₂(Θ)+m₁(Θ)m₂(F)]/(1−K); m₁₂(Θ)=m₁(Θ)m₂(Θ)/(1−K), with the corresponding expression for ¬F. Code uses a bounded denominator for numeric safety and checks that masses sum to one across the footprint.

  • Bel(F): combined mass committed to fault-supporting singleton F.
  • m(Θ): unassigned / ignorance mass. It is not conflict and does not directly map “where sources disagree.”
  • K: raw, pre-normalization conflict mass. For H58, K=0 by construction because m(not F)=0 for both sources; this does not establish agreement.
  • Pl(F)=Bel(F)+m(Θ): plausibility of F, exported separately by historical H56B.
  • H58 support difference: absolute difference between the two 300 m pre-combination support fields; this direct diagnostic is not a D-S mass.

For H58, the primary is raw Bel(F) divided by its maximum within the valid footprint. This normalizes to [0,1] but removes absolute mass scale. It is relative favorability, not a calibrated probability of fault or a validation score. Separate raw-Bel, m(Θ), K and support-difference layers are not submission alternatives. H56B's earlier zero-outside output is described in its historical report.
