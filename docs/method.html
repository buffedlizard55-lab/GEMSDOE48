1 · The official metric

Transcribed by two independent sibling repositories from the organizer's problem description (competition 306, page 967) and corroborated independently by the organizers' own reference solution, which trains with TverskyLoss(alpha=0.2, beta=0.8, mode="binary") — the metric's own weights:

k(d)  = max(1 − d / 300 m, 0)
TPw   = Σ_g max_x p(x) · k(d(x, g))
FPw   = Σ_x p(x) · (1 − max_g k(d(x, g)))
FNw   = Σ_g (1 − max_x p(x) · k(d(x, g)))
DTI   = TPw / (TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·FNw)

2 · What the metric does—and what the historical model cannot establish

The official score uses two different distance-weighted maxima: TPw = Σ_g max_x p(x)k(d(x,g)) is truth-centred, while Q = Σ_x p(x)max_g k(d(x,g)) is prediction-centred and FPw = S − Q. In general Q ≠ TPw. Do not substitute one for the other, and do not infer private-label mass or score from owner-reported leaderboard values.

8 · Dempster–Shafer terminology and total-conflict behavior

For a binary frame Θ={F,N}, a two-source normalized Dempster combination computes pre-normalization raw conflict K=m₁(F)m₂(N)+m₁(N)m₂(F), then divides every non-empty combined mass by 1−K. The resulting m₁₂(Θ)=m₁(Θ)m₂(Θ)/(1−K) is residual uncommitted/ignorance mass; it is not a direct map of source disagreement and is not K. K is normalized away and exported separately. A direct |s₁−s₂| layer is a separate support-difference diagnostic, not a D-S mass. Dempster's rule is undefined at total conflict (K=1); both src/gemsdoe48/dempster_shafer.py and the H56B-specific implementation raise ValueError at total/numerical conflict. Neither silently falls back to vacuous mass. Mathematical discount parameters are not automatically calibrated source reliabilities; using this rule does not establish statistical independence.
