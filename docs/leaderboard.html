Why 0.2778 may have scored well (hypothesis, not fact)

The official metric assigns triangular partial credit to predictions within 300 m of a fault and weights false negatives more heavily than false positives (α=0.2, β=0.8). A pruned, spacing-tuned dotted surface could therefore benefit from retaining high-credit corridor locations while removing low-credit additions, especially near a known-catalogue mask. This is a plausible mechanism consistent with the observed result, not a causal explanation. The local B2 audit says UNSCORED, and no organizer receipt links its exact SHA-256 bytes to 0.2778. The leaderboard observation does not identify what geometry or method produced the score.
