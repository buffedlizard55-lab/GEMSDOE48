Primary organizers and government data sources support factual competition, metric, science, and availability claims. Owner sites establish only their own artifacts and reported scores. A source listing is not the same as downloading, hash-checking, licensing, or validating a data file.

Evidence policy

USGS/DOE/DrivenData pages establish the referenced public facts. GitHub owner sites establish owner artifact bytes, derived proxies and owner-reported experimental scores only. A hash proves byte identity—not source truth, geology, accuracy, score attribution, submission acceptance, or scientific validity. Full source-availability notes are in source-checks.json.

Manual-review links are direct. Unknowns and failed transfers are labeled rather than inferred.
