Metric correction

The historical H55 0.2843 “ceiling”, H56B 0.0649 projection, H57-RELIEF 0.3844 projection, inferred hidden-truth masses, and universal 0.0556/0.0549-per-dot thresholds used the generally false identity FPw = S − TPw. The official metric uses prediction-centred FPw = S − Q; its false-positive and true-positive terms are not generally interchangeable. Those historical projections are invalid as score estimates or gates. Read the general metric-identity erratum and H57-RELIEF correction.
