1 · The one-paragraph version

Every previous session in this repository ranked candidates against public map proxies and then declined to submit. This session replaced the proxy with a forward model calibrated on eight owner-reported live scores. The model has two constants — the hidden truth mass |G| = 14,027.5 px and a coverage-to-truth ratio ρ = 0.068015 — and it reproduces all eight live scores with an RMS relative error of 1.758 %. Used honestly, it settles three questions that were previously matters of opinion: the 0.2778 artifact is within 1.84 % of coverage-optimal for its corridor field; the entire field is capped at live-equivalent 0.2844 at any mass; and the union of the two best families is worth -0.01398. Nothing in this repository can reach 0.2888, let alone 0.3195 or 0.3774, without a better detector.

2 · The model

k(d) = max(1 − d/300 m, 0)          official 300 m triangular kernel, α = 0.2, β = 0.8
FPw  = S − TPw ,  FNw = |G| − TPw   exact for a matched binary emission
DTI  = T / (0.2·S + 0.8·|G|)        linear in recovered truth at fixed mass

T    = ρ · Cov(X)                   Cov(X) = Σ_(b ∈ B_elig) max_(x ∈ X) k(d(x,b))
B_elig = h19-5 backbone AND >200 m from the public catalogue   (103,805 px)

|G| is identified because A_d2_8 ⊃ B_prune100 ⊃ C_prune200 are strictly nested (verified pixel-wise, not assumed) and the removed dots are catalogue-adjacent, so all three share one T. Least squares gives |G| = 14,027.5; the three rungs then invert to T = 5,210.4 / 5,216.1 / 5,209.5, agreeing to 0.11 %. That self-consistency is the strongest single piece of evidence in the repository that catalogue-adjacent dots earn zero.

3 · Why coverage, not mass, is the explanatory variable

S spans 37,654 – 121,131 (3.2×) while T spans 5,082 – 6,813. Two artifacts with the same mass (C at 37,654 and h36_rung30 at 37,660) differ 2.4 % in T and 2.7 % in Cov. Two artifacts differing 12 % in mass (C and h32_prethin_tip) differ 0.07 % in T and 0.9 % in Cov. Mass is noise; coverage of the corridor field is signal.

4 · The ceiling

Greedy maximum-coverage selection over B_elig, traced from n = 1 to n = 45,000, maximises model DTI at n = 37,167 with Cov = 76,612.8 → model DTI 0.27932 → live-equivalent 0.28435. C sits at n = 37,654 with Cov = 75,206.8, i.e. 1.84 % short of coverage-optimal and 0.4 % off the optimal mass. The whole remaining in-family headroom is +0.0066 model units against an instrument resolution of ±0.0049.

Therefore: #8 (0.2888), #7 (0.3195), #3 (0.3262), #2 (0.3345) and #1 (0.3774) are all unreachable from this corridor field at any mass — and #1 needs more truth (T = 7,077) than the dense backbone contains in total (T = 6,813).

5 · Two instruments this repository relied on are contradicted by a live score

The SGMC off-catalogue proxy. An emission built directly on it — 44,090 px, the same Poisson spacing as rung A — scored 0.0512 live, inverting to T = 1,026 against the family's 5,209 at the same mass. If that layer modelled the hidden truth, the file would have scored near 0.26.

Catalogue coverage per pixel. SGMC faults cover known faults at 0.1418 per emitted px against the backbone's 0.1007 — 41 % more efficient — and score 5× worse live. The ≥ 2× “catalogue lift” rule that rejected H52 is not a sound screen, and this session does not use it.

The one out-of-family transfer test available gives an implied ρ of 0.11025 against the family's 0.06802: the model under-predicts out-of-family T by 38.3 %. That is why H53's conduit anchors are reported as a scenario band and never as a prediction — and why the bias direction favours them.

7 · Dempster–Shafer: what it does here

Frame Θ = {F, ¬F}, Shafer reliability discounts α₁ = α₂ = 0.6 on the two families' kernel-coverage favourability surfaces. Measured on the shipped raster: Bel(F) ∈ [0.00, 0.84], Pl(F) ∈ [0.16, 1.00], m(Θ) ∈ [0.16, 0.25] and K ∈ [0.00, 0.36] with 14.70 % of the footprint in active conflict. m(Θ) attains its maximum exactly on one-sided support: the disagreement is carried forward as unassigned mass, not averaged away. All four graded layers ship separately under docs/downloads/diagnostics/.

Not the naive mean. Pearson(Bel, ½(b₁+b₂)) = 0.996429, mean |Δ| = 0.013470, max |Δ| = 0.1600, 13.71 % of the footprint differs by more than 0.05, and the best affine fit leaves a mean residual of 0.005912 — so Bel is not an affine function of the mean. The high rank correlation (Spearman 0.99995) is expected and is not evidence of equivalence: on this support both statistics are monotone in the same coverage field, so only the affine residual and the difference distribution are informative.

Why the submission is binary anyway. d/dv[(T₀ + v·k)/(D₀ + 0.2v)] has the sign of k − 0.2·DTI and is independent of v, so every cell of a graded belief surface is pushed to 0 or 1. A graded Bel submission is strictly worse than its own binarisation. The DS structure therefore enters the placement of dots (A2's pool is the disagreement set; A1 is vetoed at maximal conflict unless a third independent source arbitrates) rather than the emitted values.
