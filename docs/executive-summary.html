Metric correction

The historical H55 0.2843 “ceiling”, H56B 0.0649 projection, inferred hidden-truth masses, and universal 0.0556-per-dot threshold used the generally false identity FPw = S − TPw. The official metric uses prediction-centred FPw = S − Q; its false-positive and true-positive terms are not generally interchangeable. Those historical projections are invalid as score estimates or gates. Read the metric-identity erratum.
