What it is, in one paragraph

A Dempster–Shafer fusion of the group's best dotted file (h33-2-b2, live 0.2778) and best tip file (h32-1, live 0.2649). It emits 37,754 dots: all 37,654 of b2, plus 100 tip-family dots that the fusion accepted, with 0 dots within 200 m of the catalogue. The disagreement between the two families is published as its own layer. Verdict from the holdout: tie with 0.2778, downside bounded at ≥ 0.2773 (method). Leaderboard #1 is 0.3774. Reaching it needs new data (see hypotheses and next steps).

Diagnostic layers (not for submission, for geologists)

layer meaning
belief m(F), normalised 0–1 where the fused model believes there is a fault
conflict K where the two strongest approaches actively disagree
Yager m(Θ) unassigned belief including conflict
Dempster m(Θ) unassigned belief after normalisation

The belief layer is technically in the submission format, but it spreads mass over ~770k pixels. By the scale identity and marginal rule that would score far below the binary file. Do not submit it.
