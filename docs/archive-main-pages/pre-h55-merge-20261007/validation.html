Fold protocol (identical for all rows and both targets)

  • Four fixed 2×2 quadrants on the full 3,730 × 3,292 EPSG:32611, 100 m grid.
  • Truth is counted only in the held-out core quadrant. The evaluation domain includes that quadrant plus a 300 m Euclidean halo, clipped to the finite sample-template footprint; prediction is zero outside this domain for the metric calculation.
  • Distance-weighted Tversky uses the official triangular 300 m kernel, α=0.2 and β=0.8. Per-fold metric is calculated independently and the displayed mean is the unweighted average of four fold scores (not a pooled tier sweep).
  • All candidates are scored against the same truth mask and the same fold/domain arrays. Prediction surfaces are not retrained or rebuilt per fold.

Leakage and inference limits

  • The H33-2-B2 owner audit describes deleting candidate predictions within 200 m of the full public catalogue. Source construction therefore had access to catalogue geometry that includes held-out areas. The source surfaces were not fold-rebuilt, so this is a conditional comparison, not leakage-free generalization.
  • The SGMC proxy removes cells within 300 m of catalogue labels, but this filter does not undo the source-generation leakage or create private expert truth.
  • The mirrored candidate, label, sample-template and SGMC TIFFs are not organizer-authenticated; the source-mirror reuse/license status is not verified. The underlying SGMC catalog is official USGS public data, but this processed raster's lineage was not rebuilt here.
  • No leaderboard score, hidden-test estimate, or private label result is available from these diagnostics.
