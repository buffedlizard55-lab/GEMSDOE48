# Five prospective geological hypotheses — none slot-cleared

**Decision context.** The requested H48 Yager fusion failed the corrected SGMC proxy gate. The prior H48-1 Dempster candidate also did not beat b2 on its reported spatial holdout; LSI estimates are not precise enough to claim a tie. The following are new, falsifiable data/transform hypotheses, not validated candidate submissions. Ranking is qualitative relative upside per implementation cost. **No numeric ΔDTI forecast is justified.** No weekly slot may be used until a candidate beats the corrected holdout best and clears the written raster-format gate.

Novelty here is **operational and checkout-scoped**, not a claim that no related concept has ever been tried. The owner’s full site inventory in the README records prior LiDAR scarp/ridge work ([GEMSDOE7](https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/7GEMSDOE/)), combined topo-geophysical work ([GEMSDOE16](https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/16GEMSDOE/docs/index.html)), and scarp–radiometric work ([GEMSDOE46](https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/GEMSDOE46/)). N1, N2, and N5 therefore name specific transforms/controls that were not implemented in this checkout, not wholly unprecedented data families. The old H47-B TMI-up150 multi-scale persistence arm is not counted as new: the [owner-reported public-catalogue screen](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE47/blob/main/docs/validation-h47b-20261006.md) says its pooled locked-block proxy DTI (0.02755) lost to its fixed-seed random control (0.03716). Those are not organizer/private scores or independent replication.

## Promotion and validation protocol

1. Select one detector and freeze layers, processing, confounds, thresholds, spatial folds, and promotion rule **before** looking at held-out proxy labels.
2. Obtain/check official data rights, checksums, CRS, footprint, tile seams, survey provenance, and compatibility with contest terms. A catalog entry or count is not a validated raster.
3. Generate fold-specific predictions without using held-out labels; compare with the corrected tip, dotted, and naïve-mean anchors using `src/gems48/validation.py` and exact known-fault masking.
4. Require an improvement over the best baseline and consistent blocked folds. Public SGMC proxy gains are necessary evidence only, not proof of private-score gain.
5. Re-open the final GeoTIFF, verify its [0,1] range, and resolve the official null/NaN-outside vs portal range-check conflict before any upload. **No candidate on this page is slot-cleared.**
