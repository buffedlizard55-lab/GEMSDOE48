How to prepare a future candidate

  1. Confirm that the candidate addresses a pre-ranked geological hypothesis and has a frozen, reproducible spatially blocked holdout protocol.
  2. Require a comparable improvement over the current holdout best on the leading public proxy and examine fold-level variation and matched controls. A proxy result is still not a private-label score.
  3. Check feature-source coverage, provenance, licensing and challenge-use terms. Do not assume a publicly downloadable asset is complete or permitted for this use.
  4. Build one unique, one-band float32 GeoTIFF on the official grid. Run the local format validator on the exact bytes and save the SHA-256.
  5. Check the current competition rules and weekly submission allowance. Keep “OK to download for inspection” separate from “OK to submit”; only submit when the current project gate explicitly says OK TO SUBMIT.
  6. Use the candidate-specific unique name and short note, and retain the organizer's resulting score/receipt. Only organizer-provided evidence can establish competition performance.

H50's local format record

The H50 GeoTIFF passed the historical local checks for one float32 band, EPSG:32611, 100 m pixels, 3730×3292 grid and in-footprint range [0,1]. What the diagnostics mean

m(Θ) is ignorance/unassigned mass; K is conflict. H50 stores these as distinct diagnostics under normalized Dempster combination. Neither is a fault probability or score. Details are in the metric and method erratum.
