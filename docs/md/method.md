# Current metric and evidence method

> Earlier alpha=.99 / DS48 Markdown method prose is preserved in [`docs/archive-main-pages/md/method.md`](../archive-main-pages/md/method.md). Use the maintained [method page](../method.html) and [metric-identity erratum](../research/metric-identity-erratum-20261007.md) for current equations and claims.

The official metric uses truth-centred `TPw=T` and prediction-centred `FPw=S−Q`; `T` and `Q` maximize over different axes, so `FPw=S−TPw` is not a general identity. Historical inversions and their ceilings, thresholds, hidden-truth counts, and score scenarios are invalid for decisions.

For Dempster–Shafer diagnostics, `m(Θ)` is residual ignorance/unassigned mass. `K` is raw conjunctive conflict, distinct from `m(Θ)`; normalized Dempster combination does not transfer `K` into ignorance. Publish the layers separately and do not interpret them as calibrated probabilities.
