Remaining work · limitations · ordered priorities

What is left, and what blocks it

The one thing that would change the outcome

Every leaderboard position above 0.2778 is unreachable from the h19-5 corridor field at any mass. The frontier maximum is 0.2844 live-equivalent. #8 needs T = 5,416 and #1 needs T = 7,077, against a dense-field total of 6,813. The remaining work is therefore not better combination, better spacing or better pruning — all three are provably within 2 % of exhausted. It is a better detector.

Ordered priorities

  1. Submit H53 and record the returned score. Cost: one weekly slot. Value: it is the only experiment available that can test the hydrothermal-conduit hypothesis, and the arithmetic was built so the answer is legible — materially above 0.2778 means conduits carry credit, near 0.2727 means they do not. Worst case -0.00507.
  2. Spend one slot on a DENSE emission of a second corridor field. This is the highest-information slot in the project. A dense (unthinned) emission of any new field inverts directly to that field's total truth yield T under the calibrated |G|, and simultaneously re-calibrates ρ for a second family — converting the instrument from within-family to cross-family and making every later candidate priceable offline. The best first field is H53-B (strain-rate localisation ridges), because bands 4/7/8/16 are measured here to be close to pure unused information (dot lift 1.03 – 1.08).
  3. Implement H53-B. Data is in hand and byte-verified. Multi-scale vesselness / Sato ridge filter plus structure-tensor coherence on log(second invariant) and shear rate at 300 – 900 m; keep coherence above the 95th percentile; emit with the existing greedy coverage rule and the existing pre-registered budget. Cost low-medium.
  4. Unblock H53-D. Verify the units of facing_at against strike_at in src/gemsdoe48/scarp3m.py and data/external/h52_scarp3m_merge_log.txt before building any along-strike walk — the measured median perpendicularity is 46°, not ~0°, so one of the two is not what its name says. Then re-test lidar scarps as traces rather than points. Highest ceiling of any candidate.
  5. Implement H53-C. Basement-depth second derivative × isostatic-gravity horizontal gradient × conductivity contrast. Targets a measured blind spot: C's dots sit on shallow basement (402 m vs 535 m) and on topographic highs (+7.3 vs −15.6), so the family systematically misses buried basin-margin faults. Data in hand; cost low.
  6. Ride H53-E on the result of step 1. Three-source Dempster conjunction (Quaternary trace with a recorded slip rate × conduit tier × strain ridge). Small mass, small in both directions, and its prior probability depends entirely on whether step 1 says conduit evidence carries credit.
  7. Stop spending slots on spacing, pruning and fusion sweeps. §3.1 of the report bounds all three. The rung-D 200 – 300 m catalogue prune in particular is an irreversible removal against a Phase-2 truth that the organizers expand by expert review of all Phase-1 submissions.
