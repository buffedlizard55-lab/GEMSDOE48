Dempster–Shafer diagnostics

The H56B artifact combines discounted evidence with normalized Dempster's rule. The normalized Bel(F) surface is scaled by its in-footprint maximum and is a relative favorability surface, not a calibrated fault probability. Diagnostic layers are separate: m(Θ) is residual unassigned/ignorance mass; raw K is conflict. m(Θ) is not “where the families disagree.” For H56-OWDS, direct absolute support-surface difference is also provided as a separate, non-mass diagnostic.

Metric correction

Do not use historical live-score projections. The former H55/H56 calculations assume `FPw = S − TPw`. The official metric uses prediction-centred `FPw = S − Q`, while `TPw = T` is truth-centred; the maxima are over different axes. The old H55 0.2843 ceiling, H56B 0.0649 projection, inferred hidden-truth counts, and universal 0.0556 threshold are not valid score estimates or gates. See the derivation and executable counterexample.
