The mechanism, arithmetically. With the official identity FNw = |G| − TPw, the metric is DTI = TPw / (0.2·TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·|G|). Deleting one unit of mass lowers the denominator by 0.2·(1 − k) and the numerator by the credit it was earning. So a dot is worth emitting iff its realised kernel weight exceeds 0.2·DTI — 0.0556 at 0.2778. The measured live break-even is 0.05416 credit per dot. Full derivation: Why 0.2778 won.
