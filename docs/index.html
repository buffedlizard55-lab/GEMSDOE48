Dempster–Shafer diagnostics

H58-A combines positive-only simple-support masses with normalized Dempster's rule. Its normalized Bel(F) is relative favorability, not a calibrated fault probability. H58 assigns m(not F)=0, so raw conflict K is identically zero by construction; this is not evidence of agreement. m(Θ) is unassigned/ignorance, not “where the families disagree.” Direct absolute parent-support difference is exported as a separate non-mass diagnostic. Metric correction

Do not use historical live-score projections. The former H55/H56 calculations assume `FPw = S − TPw`. The official metric uses prediction-centred `FPw = S − Q`, while `TPw = T` is truth-centred; the maxima are over different axes. The old H55 0.2843 ceiling, H56B 0.0649 projection, inferred hidden-truth counts, and universal 0.0556 threshold are not valid score estimates or gates. See the derivation and executable counterexample.
