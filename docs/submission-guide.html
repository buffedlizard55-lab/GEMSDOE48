Upload steps — only after a candidate clears the research gate

  1. Choose the exact approved artifact. Download one single-band GeoTIFF from the project's executive summary. Check that the candidate's status explicitly says it is cleared for consideration. The current H50-GDR file fails that condition; do not upload it.
  2. Sign in to DrivenData and open the DOE GEMS Prize Challenge. Read the official problem and submission-format page and current competition rules. This repository cannot bypass login or upload on your behalf.
  3. Open the competition's submission form. Choose “File to submit”, select the downloaded .tif (or a ZIP containing exactly one TIFF if the portal requires it), and paste the short candidate comment in the optional note field. Do not upload diagnostic TIFFs or multiple files.
  4. Review and submit in the portal. Wait for DrivenData's file validation. Local validation does not guarantee portal acceptance. If the portal reports a CRS, shape, geotransform, nodata, or range error, stop and preserve the exact error text; do not spend another slot on a modified guess.
  5. Record the outcome. Save the submission ID, timestamp, exact filename, note, and displayed score in the project log. Do not attribute a leaderboard row to a TIFF unless the organizer explicitly links that score to the artifact.
