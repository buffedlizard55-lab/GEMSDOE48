How to read the record

  • Public-map proxy scores are not private competition truth, leaderboard results or expected private scores. Frozen upstream features were not reconstructed inside every holdout fold; they may leak.
  • For H58, `m(Θ)` denotes unassigned/ignorance and raw conflict `K=0` by construction because both sources assign zero `m(not F)`; this is not evidence of agreement. H58's direct support difference is a separate diagnostic, not a mass.
  • The 0.2778 leaderboard entry and owner-reported family ladder are not linked by organizer receipt to exact local B2 TIFF bytes; the local file is recorded `UNSCORED`. Staff clarified the known-fault mask is pixel-exact with no 300 m buffer.
  • H55/H56 live-score surrogate values based on `FPw=S−TPw` are invalid historical outputs. The correct identity is `FPw=S−Q`, where Q and T are maxima over different axes.
  • The inventory is reproducible, but this checkout lacks a full supervised train→inference pipeline. Restoring one third-party mirror does not fill that gap or authenticate competition provenance.
