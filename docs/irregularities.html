Current corrections and caveats

Metric identity: live projections invalid

The official terms are TPw=T, FPw=S−Q, FNw=N−T. The prediction-centred maximum Q and truth-centred maximum T generally differ. Therefore the H55/H56 substitutions using FPw=S−TPw are invalid; the 0.2843 “ceiling,” 0.0649 H56B projection, inferred hidden-positive totals, universal per-dot threshold and reachability claims are not score estimates or valid promotion evidence.

Ignorance, conflict and direct difference are distinct

For H58, m(Θ) is residual unassigned/ignorance mass, not conflict or a family-disagreement map. K is raw pre-normalization conflict and is identically zero by construction because both sources set m(not F)=0; this is not evidence of agreement. Absolute parent-support difference is a separate non-mass diagnostic. The B2 and H33-D positive masks overlap on 31,614 cells, so their statistical independence is not established.
