## Construction and evidence semantics

The preregistered parent rasters are the owner-mirror H33-2-B2 dotted mask (SHA-256
`c55bafc470054e8271dcb89347a17e07fefe50de6af6e6ba6c4b169ef7ab6fa9`) and H33-D tip/step-over
mask (SHA-256 `87f857d505e23247e991ccfab2cbe9f49a04df4f9c8028dce7ea261554690757`). The owner-
reported 0.2778 and 0.2632 scores are not verified for these exact local bytes. The parents have
37,654 and 41,865 positive cells, with 31,614 shared (positive-mask Jaccard 0.65993); separate
construction histories do **not** establish independent evidence.

For each parent, H58 computes its metric-support surface `sᵢ(x)=max(1−d(x,Dᵢ)/300m,0)` on the
100 m grid and assigns fixed positive-only simple-support masses:

```text
mᵢ(F)     = 0.60 sᵢ
mᵢ(not F) = 0
mᵢ(Theta) = 1 − 0.60 sᵢ
```

Normalized Dempster combination then gives `Bel(F)=f₁+f₂−f₁f₂` and
`m(Theta)=u₁u₂`. Raw conflict `K=0` **by construction** because neither source assigns mass to
`not F`; this is not evidence that the parents agree. The normalized primary is raw Bel(F)
divided by its maximum within the competition footprint. It is relative favorability, not a
calibrated probability. `m(Theta)` is residual ignorance/unassigned mass, not a disagreement
map. H58 exports it separately, along with raw Bel(F), raw K and an actual support-difference
raster `abs(s₁−s₂)`. The latter is a direct support comparison, not a D-S mass.

- Raw Bel(F) diagnostic: [`belief-raw.tif`](../downloads/diagnostics/GEMSDOE48-H58-OWDS-POSONLY-B2xH33D-20261008-fdbb83476756-belief-raw.tif)
- Unassigned `m(Theta)` diagnostic: [`unassigned-mtheta.tif`](../downloads/diagnostics/GEMSDOE48-H58-OWDS-POSONLY-B2xH33D-20261008-fdbb83476756-unassigned-mtheta.tif)
- Conflict `K` diagnostic (identically zero): [`conflict-k.tif`](../downloads/diagnostics/GEMSDOE48-H58-OWDS-POSONLY-B2xH33D-20261008-fdbb83476756-conflict-k.tif)
- Direct support difference: [`support-difference.tif`](../downloads/diagnostics/GEMSDOE48-H58-OWDS-POSONLY-B2xH33D-20261008-fdbb83476756-support-difference.tif)

These four diagnostics are research layers, **not alternate submissions**. In the footprint,
`Bel(F)` spans 0 to 0.84000003, normalized Bel spans 0 to 1, mean `m(Theta)` is 0.93737, and
maximum absolute mass-sum error is 1.2e-7.

## What 0.2778 and 0.3195 mean — careful answer

A live read of the [official public leaderboard](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/leaderboard/)
on **2026-10-08 UTC** showed #1 at 0.3774, #2 at 0.3345, #3 at 0.3262, #4 at 0.3260,
#5 at 0.3222, #6 at 0.3221, **0.3195 at #7 (DARD)**, and the **0.2778 row at #13
(`extradr19`)**. This is a dated public-board observation, not an organizer receipt for a
particular file and not the private test result; the board can change. Machine-readable snapshot:
[`docs/data/leaderboard_20261008.json`](../data/leaderboard_20261008.json).

The local H33-2-B2 bytes are SHA-256
`c55bafc470054e8271dcb89347a17e07fefe50de6af6e6ba6c4b169ef7ab6fa9`; their local audit says
`UNSCORED`. The 0.2778 number is an owner-reported family score and also appears on the public
board under `extradr19`, but no receipt links that row or the score to these exact bytes. The
reported 0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778 progression and 37,654-dot count are leads, not verified local
measurements or a causal experiment.

A current [DrivenData staff clarification](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4)
settles an important geometry point: only the exact pixels corresponding to the provided
training-fault labels are masked; there is no 300 m buffer around the known traces. Predictions
one to three pixels away are scored normally against *new*-fault truth, and a new fault may lie
within 300 m of a known trace. Therefore it is not sound to explain the reported ladder as
“near-catalogue predictions are free” or as a direct benefit from a 200–300 m scoring mask. A
pruning operation could still change false-positive mass, spatial coverage and hits relative to
the hidden new faults, but its direction/effect cannot be reconstructed without exact files,
labels, submitted artifact receipts and scorer inputs. **The reason for 0.2778 remains unknown.**

The official problem description says the test targets are newly identified expert-labelled
faults and defines the private/public competition structure; the final prize round uses expert-
verified additions. Consequently, neither local H49's 0.095353/0.100751 public-proxy means nor
the visible 0.3195 row can be directly compared to the private score or used as a private-score
prediction for H58.
