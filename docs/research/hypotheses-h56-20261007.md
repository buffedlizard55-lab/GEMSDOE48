# Archived H56 hypothesis slate — historical record, not current advice

> **Correction (2026-10-07):** This slate predates H57 and is not the current implementation plan. Its hidden-truth estimates, live-equivalent projections, 0.0556 universal per-cell bar, modeled expected ΔDTI, family ceilings, and reachability claims that rely on `FPw=S−TPw` are withdrawn. Public-proxy DTI values remain direct measurements of the stated public layers only; they do not establish private-label performance or organizer acceptance. Candidate chronology and recipe issues are documented in the [H56B review erratum](h56b-review-erratum-20261007.md).

- The H56 machine slate has five entries and is preserved at [`evidence/hypothesis_slate_h56_20261007.json`](../../evidence/hypothesis_slate_h56_20261007.json). Its original candidate descriptions and source caveats are historical metadata, not validated current hypotheses.

The prose-only structure-coherence extension was not implemented/tested. See [`evidence/holdout_h56a_strain_ridge_20261007.json`](../../evidence/holdout_h56a_strain_ridge_20261007.json).
- The H56B-specific slate's rank is not a verified preregistration of final H56B; its declared freeze is after an earlier predecessor result and it describes a different recipe. H56B-NF was built post hoc. See [`evidence/h56_preregistration_reconciliation_20261007.json`](../../evidence/h56_preregistration_reconciliation_20261007.json) and the [review corrections](../../evidence/h56b_review_corrections_20261007.json).
- Current H56B-NF loses to H49 on all four folds for both public proxies. That is a negative result for this tested artifact/protocol, not a private-label prediction. No weekly slot is cleared.

## Current plan

The current five unbuilt, previously untried hypotheses are H57-A through H57-E, documented with input layers, target physical signature, off-catalogue rationale, prior-art distinction, expected DTI impact, cost, and source availability in the [H57 slate](h57-hypothesis-slate-20261007.md) and its [machine-readable record](../../evidence/hypothesis_slate_h57_20261007.json). Freeze a spatially blocked holdout before implementation; do not spend a slot unless the candidate beats the comparable blocked best. Public-proxy success is necessary for consideration, not evidence of organizer score or acceptance.

Read the [metric-identity erratum](metric-identity-erratum-20261007.md) before interpreting any historical score conversion.
