## Prior-art and evidence boundary

- Local candidate parents are the owner-mirrored H33-2-B2 dotted raster (SHA-256 `c55bafc470054e8271dcb89347a17e07fefe50de6af6e6ba6c4b169ef7ab6fa9`) and H33-D tip/step-over raster (SHA-256 `87f857d505e23247e991ccfab2cbe9f49a04df4f9c8028dce7ea261554690757`). Their reported 0.2778 and 0.2632 scores are **not** verified as scores for these exact local bytes; the local B2 audit says `UNSCORED`. See the [attribution analysis](why-02778-and-ceiling-20261007.md), [B2 audit](../../data/raw/audit-h33-2-b2.json), and [source audit](../../evidence/source_audit.json).
- The two parent supports overlap substantially. Prior artifact receipts report 31,614 overlapping positive cells; this is not evidence of independent sources. The parent files are frozen outputs, not fold-local reconstructions.
- Dempster–Shafer (D-S) is a combining framework, not new geology. Earlier repository candidates already combined the B2/H33-D pair using symmetric and open-world mass assignments. H58-A is specifically a new **positive-only simple-support** assignment; it is not a new sensor, fault detector, or claim of source independence.
- `m(Θ)` is residual unassigned/ignorance mass. It is not itself a direct disagreement statistic. Raw D-S conflict `K` is distinct. H58-A deliberately assigns no negative mass to “not emitted” cells, so its D-S `K` is exactly zero; a separate absolute parent-support difference is required to display where the two maps disagree.

## Frozen H58-A construction and test

1. Inputs: the two SHA-pinned parent rasters above, plus the SHA-pinned 100 m footprint mask. Restrict parent positives and all emitted predictions to the finite footprint.
2. Convert each binary parent mask `Dᵢ` to `sᵢ(x)=max(1−d(x,Dᵢ)/300 m,0)` with 100 m Euclidean grid spacing.
3. Assign positive simple-support masses with fixed `α₁=α₂=0.60`:
   `mᵢ(F)=αsᵢ`, `mᵢ(N)=0`, `mᵢ(Θ)=1−αsᵢ`.
4. Combine by normalized Dempster's rule. Since neither source assigns mass to `N`, `K=0` and the exact combination is `Bel(F)=m₁(F)+m₂(F)−m₁(F)m₂(F)` and `m₁₂(Θ)=m₁(Θ)m₂(Θ)`. Assert the masses sum to one. Export relative `Bel(F)/max_footprint(Bel(F))` as the primary `[0,1]` surface; export unnormalized `Bel(F)`, residual `m(Θ)`, `K`, and `abs(s₁−s₂)` as separate diagnostics. The latter is the direct disagreement layer, not a D-S mass.
5. Compare the candidate with both parents, their naive arithmetic mean, their union, and the same-protocol H49 reference on the existing four fixed geographic quadrants. Score only truth in each quadrant core, using the core plus a 300 m halo clipped to footprint and the official distance-weighted Tversky parameters. Evaluate (a) the public catalogue raster and (b) the newer SGMC fault raster restricted to cells strictly more than 300 m from catalogue positives. Also report the older raw-SGMC raster separately as sensitivity, never pooled.
6. **No slot rule:** H58-A must exceed H49 in mean DTI and win at least 3/4 paired folds on both proxy targets to pass this *necessary* research gate. A pass would still not establish private-label/leaderboard gain, source independence, organizer file acceptance, or license clearance. A fail means no weekly submission. No portal action is authorized by this slate.
