# H56 results: open-world D-S deliverable and failed strain-ridge test — 2026-10-07

## Decision in one sentence

**The H56 GeoTIFF is built and locally format-audited; it is OK to download for inspection, but do not submit it or spend a weekly slot.** Its open-world Dempster–Shafer (D-S) Bel(F) surface is a byte- and pixel-distinct *parameterization* of family fusion already present in H53, not a new geological evidence family. It fails to beat H49 on both same-protocol public proxies, and the machine-spec H56-A Hessian-only screen fails by a wider margin. The original prose slate additionally mentioned an unparameterized coherence term; that broader variant remains untested, as recorded in the erratum. No private-label score, organizer score, upload, or organizer acceptance exists.

## What the D-S result is—and is not

The two parents are SHA-pinned local mirrors of H33-2-B2 dotted (37,654 positive mask cells) and the actual H33-D tip/step-over family (41,865 positive mask cells). Each sparse mask is converted to the competition's 300 m triangular maximum-credit support field `b`. Their intersection is 31,614 cells and union 47,905 cells (Jaccard `0.65993`), so high statistical dependence is plausible; separate source pipelines do not establish independent evidence.

The frozen open-world assignment for each source is

```text
m(F)     = alpha * b
m(not F) = alpha * q * (1 - b)
m(Theta) = 1 - m(F) - m(not F)
alpha = 0.60, q = 0.10
```

Only 10% of the source-weighted lack of support is allocated to `not F`; the remainder, plus the reliability discount, stays uncommitted on `Theta`. `q` and `alpha` were not learned from hidden labels or calibrated on a private target. The two BPAs are combined pixelwise by normalized Dempster's rule. The primary is raw `Bel(F)` divided by its in-footprint maximum (0.84), so it is a `[0,1]` **relative favorability field**, not a calibrated fault probability or a literal BPA mass. The raw Bel, residual `m(Theta)`, conflict `K`, plausibility, and parent-support difference remain separately downloadable. An independent diagnostic audit verifies `primary = Bel/max(Bel)`, `Pl=Bel+m(Theta)`, finite bounded layers, and distinct `m(Theta)`/`K` rasters.

### Novelty audit: the combination idea already existed

H53 already built the same B2 × H33-D parent pair and exported a two-family D-S diagnostic inside its H53-RadEdge work; its full H53 submission adds GeoDAWN radiometric edges. The H53 family simple-support assignment uses reliability `0.50` with `m(F)=alpha*b`, `m(not F)=alpha*(1-b)`, and `m(Theta)=1-alpha`. H56 instead freezes `alpha=0.60` and `q=0.10` for both sparse parents, which assigns much less negative mass to an omitted/unsupported cell. H56 is therefore a distinct, audited open-world parameterization and file—not a relabelled H53 TIFF—but **not a novel D-S combination concept, not a new geological signal, and not evidence that the families are independent**. D-S diagnostics also appear in DS48 and H55.

The local audit searched `data/` and `docs/downloads/`: 85 earlier same-grid, one-band rasters were compared inside the footprint; none has the H56 SHA-256 or pixelwise-equal values. That proves only bounded local distinction, not global uniqueness. The direct H53 two-family D-S diagnostic has Pearson `0.99054`, sampled Spearman `0.99993`, mean absolute difference `0.01526`, and top-37,654 rank Jaccard `1.0` against H56. Against the two-support arithmetic mean, H56 is **not pixelwise equal** (Pearson `0.99668`, sampled Spearman `0.999987`, mean absolute difference `0.01126`, maximum difference `0.19650`, and 12.52% of footprint cells differ by more than `0.05`), but its top-37,654 selection is also identical (Jaccard `1.0`). Thus the value raster is not the arithmetic mean, but it does **not** materially change the rank ordering at that budget. This limitation is explicit; no novelty claim is based on the high-correlation output.

## What the reported 0.2778 does and does not establish

The official [problem/metric page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) defines distance-weighted fault scoring. The official [organizer forum clarification](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4) says known USGS/INGENIOUS faults are masked from scoring. The locally preserved owner-reported dotted-family ladder is A: 44,090 dots / `0.2600`; B: 40,199 / `0.2708` after 3,891 catalogue-adjacent removals; C: 37,654 / `0.2778` after 2,545 more removals. Under the recorded metric algebra, this increase is **consistent with** removing predictions near the known catalogue that receive little/no true-positive credit under the scoring setup. The ladder is not independent proof of that mechanism.

Crucially, `registry/live_scores.json` is an owner-maintained association, not an organizer file-to-score receipt. The exact local B2 TIFF has no verified organizer receipt; its local audit calls it `UNSCORED` and gives a `0.2747` projection. The leaderboard snapshot records a `0.2778` row, but a score row does not identify these local bytes. The safe conclusion is: **0.2778 is a reported family/leaderboard-associated result; catalogue-distance pruning is a plausible, algebraically consistent explanation; neither the exact local file attribution nor any H56 score is organizer-verified.** See the calculation and assumptions in [`why-02778-and-ceiling-20261007.md`](why-02778-and-ceiling-20261007.md), [`evidence/source_audit.json`](../../evidence/source_audit.json), and the dated [`leaderboard snapshot`](../data/leaderboard_20261007.json).
