# H57-A results — radiometric K residual × magnetic gradient

> **Namespace note.** This report covers the current branch's 21:18 UTC H57-A slate only. It is
> distinct from the earlier H57-A–E planning slate and the concurrent H57-RELIEF artifact. This
> H57-A screen emitted no TIFF; H57-RELIEF is a separate built file with its own metric erratum.

**Decision: H57-A did not clear the preregistered public-proxy gate. No weekly slot; this H57-A screen generated no TIFF.** The frozen candidate slightly raised the catalogue-proxy mean but lowered the SGMC off-catalogue mean, and its ranking did not beat same-pool random selections in eight descriptive controls. Stop this transform here; do not tune it against the same proxies.

## Preregistered candidate and sources

The H57 slate was frozen before this build in [`hypotheses-h57-20261007.md`](hypotheses-h57-20261007.md) and [`evidence/hypothesis_slate_h57a_20261007.json`](../../evidence/hypothesis_slate_h57a_20261007.json). K and Th came from the local four-band uint8 GeoDAWN mirror; `TMI_up150` was restored from the hash-pinned owner mirror. Both are associated with the official [USGS GeoDAWN release, DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ](https://doi.org/10.5066/P93LGLVQ), but the local mirror is not organizer-authenticated and this run did not establish challenge-specific sponsor-sharing terms. The extension restored successfully at SHA-256 `a35a9c6d2a14786f4dab85481ee59769213072f5dab5b2535ea82ae4d9bb7d9b`; its four bands are `ThK`, `UK`, `UTh`, and `TMI_up150` on the expected EPSG:32611, 100 m grid.

The frozen transform fit five-iteration Huber IRLS K~Th, required residual z≥2 and nonzero TMI-up150 gradient ≥ its valid-domain p75, ranked by residual-z × gradient, then added the top 2,000 cells at value 1 to the unchanged H49 reference. Candidate selection used no catalogue geometry or holdout truth; the H49 reference itself is a previously constructed, frozen surface that did use public catalogue-derived evidence. No private expert labels or leaderboard values were used.

The preregistered rule requires a positive mean and at least 3/4 positive folds on *both* proxy regimes. H57-A fails; the slight catalogue-proxy increase is not a basis to submit.

## Same-pool random-addition control

Eight fixed-seed random samples of 2,000 cells from the same 17,663-cell admissible feature pool scored above the feature-ranked additions on both proxy means:

| Proxy truth | H57-A feature-ranked | Random-control mean | H57-A − random mean | Random draws ≤ H57-A |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Catalogue labels | 0.095659 | 0.096138 | −0.000479 | 0/8 |
| SGMC off-catalogue | 0.100528 | 0.100975 | −0.000447 | 0/8 |

This small control set is descriptive, not a significance test. It does not support an inference that the K-residual × magnetic-gradient ranking adds value over the admissible pool.

## Reproducibility and limitations

- Full evidence: [`evidence/holdout_h57a_20261007.json`](../../evidence/holdout_h57a_20261007.json).
- Rebuild and score: `./.venv/bin/python scripts/validate_h57a.py`.
- Unit coverage: `tests/test_h57a.py`.
- Feature fit: 5,146,342 valid cells; Huber intercept 20.4053 and slope 0.8197 in quantized units; residual MAD scale 34.6344; gradient p75 0.0474342 quantized units/m.
- Candidate: 2,000 new cells; 49,905 positive cells after adding to H49. SHA-256 of canonical float32 candidate array: `394a11c2eff65824afdfe6cf04da635a08d2741d70f906428aee3e6bdfa9b491`. The logical array is recorded for reproducibility; it is not an exported or downloadable GeoTIFF.
- GeoDAWN uint8 channels are relative quantizations, not physical-unit radiometry/magnetics; residuals and gradients can still track lithology or data-processing edges rather than faults.
- Unsupervised thresholds were estimated across the full feature grid. This is not a fold-local refit, and H49 was not rebuilt inside folds. The holdout is conditional on fixed upstream surfaces.
- The labels and SGMC faults are public-map proxies, not the private expert-labelled competition target. A leaderboard score cannot identify the file bytes without an organizer receipt.

**Clear verdict:** the existing H56B file remains **OK to download for inspection** but **NOT OK / NOT CLEARED to submit**. H57-A created no submission. Neither experiment has a verified organizer score or portal-acceptance receipt.
